## Rules that matter to us

- All code and design assets made between noon Saturday and noon Sunday. Planning,
  installing software and making accounts beforehand is allowed.
- Public libraries and packages are allowed.
- Teams of up to 4, all in person, registered on https://portal.sfusurge.com.
- The submission needs a link to the project (GitHub repo) and a demo video up to
  3 minutes. A project still marked Draft is not submitted.
- Judging: 3-minute pitch and 1-minute Q&A, up to two judging rounds. 1 to 2 presenters.
- Criteria: technical complexity, design, pitch, originality.
- Under 19: online info form; overnight needs a parent-signed paper consent form at
  check-in; not overnight means meeting the organizer outside the SUB Social Stage at
  8:00 PM Saturday, or the team is disqualified.
